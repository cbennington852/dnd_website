
## 1. Investigate the Unnatural
Gain an extra 30% to your unnatural stat.

## 2. Unnatural Developments I
Choose one of the following to add. Note that this can allow you to go over your max in any of the base stats. 

| Development Name | Description                                   |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Extra Muscles    | + 20% to Strength<br>- 10% to Charisma        |
| Extra Appendages | + 20% to Dexterity<br>-10% to Constitution    |
| Permanent Scars  | + 20% to Constitution<br>- 10% to Intellgence |
## 3. Delve into madness

As a bonus home scene you can now do the "staying on the case" action, in addition to a normal home scene.

## 4. Unnatural Developments II 
Choose one of the following to add. Note that this can allow you to go over your max in any of the base stats. 

| Development Name         | Description                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Exaggerated Traits       | Gain several retractable claws, horns, or spurs. Your unarmed melee is now deals 1d12 of damage. Melee sneak attacks are at +20% as well.                                                       |
| Stretchable Appendages   | Your Limbs can how stretch unnaturally long. On a successful Dexterity check, you can stretch your limbs up to 30ft long. Note that this can be used to grab weapons out of an opponents hands. |
| Accelerated Regeneration | Your body heals broken bones, cuts and scrapes impossibly fast. Regen 2hp per turn during combat.                                                                                               |
## 5. Numb mind
Gain an +30% bonus when rolling sanity.
