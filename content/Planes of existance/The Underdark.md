The underdark is a underground location, buried deep within the earth, the walls hear pulse orange, and this area requires a flashlight to see properly. Long hallways stretch in many directions, and a almost alien society lurks below. 
# Creatures
* [[Shog`a-lite]]  Act as citizens. 
* [[Shog`a-wrath]] Act as guards. If alarm triggered, ~1-2 will teleport in to fight. 
* [[Shog`a-neir]] Act maintenance + fixing things

# Locations
## Mushroom fields
A large cavern looms ahead of the player, small rivers run through large feilds of mushrooms. The room smells of spores, and stretched on for (2d4) football feilds. 

* Players get a +20% bonus to sneaking if they hide in the mushrooms. 

__Occupants__
* Generally 1d4 of the [[Shog`a-lite]]
* One [[Shog`a-neir]] fixing a aqueduct. 

__Eating a mushroom__
* Roll Luck 
	* Pass ... Safe
	* Fail ... Constitution check

## Containment rooms
A small room that contains human children in test tubes. These have (1d4) children in test tubes, and small rock cabinets and desks nearby. 

On a failed luck roll this can contain a [[Shog`a-neir]] and a [[Shog`a-puter]]

__The children__
All of them are in a coma, rescuing them would require the agents to carry them to surface and get to hospital, all of them can be traced back to missing persons cases. 

__Cabinets__
Contains small obsidian scalpels and other rock related tools. 

## Train Stations & Trains 
__Occupants__
* One [[Shog`a-puter]] to control trains.


## Living Quarters
A small "sleeping" space for the Shog's to sleep and recharge. Oh, and also do things. 

The wall pulse especially orange here, the rock is soft and malleable, similar to how leather would feel. Inside of this room are small "tubes" that connect to the back of the shog's heads. 

__Occupants__
* Generally 1d4 of the [[Shog`a-lite]]
* Luck Roll (to determine who is awake)
