## Hook
The handler calls the team to his RV at 9pm at night on a friday. They have located a wizard who is suspected of turning people and small dogs into shrimp. Through sheer luck, we managed to get a copy of his bus ticket out of town.


## Objectives
A suspected Wizard will board a Metro Bus(east bound Division 25 stop 3 @ 1:23 am). Bring him in alive or dead. Make sure to bring in any items he has on his persons. 

## The Bus
The bus will arrive around 1:23am. It is cold and gloomy, the only light around them is from streetlights. 

## People on the bus
As the players get on the bus you can see there are two total people on the bus.... Well, three if you count the bus driver. 

### The Grumpy Elderly Man
A grumpy elderly man, will snarl at anyone nearby.  He is nearly black out drunk, and reeks of alcohol.  He is spread out, taking up two bus seats in the disabled section. You can see two bottles of beer under his left arm. 

__Stats__
HP : 12
* Revolver (60% firearms) 1d8
* Unarmed (40%)
* All other stats (30%)

### The bird lover
A young white male. His is watching some kind of anime on his phone, with the phone speaker on full blast. He is also wearing a red angry birds hat. If engaged in conversation he will adamantly talk about his favorite game, which is about slingshotting birds into houses constructed about pigs. (I can't say what game due to copyright)
__Stats__
HP : 10
* Unarmed (40%)
* All other stats (30%)

### The Bus Driver
The bus driver is not paying attention, and is looking down and is scrolling on his phone. He does not acknowledge the players as they get on the bus. He is wearing a bus drivers uniform, and is middled aged and balding.

__Stats__
HP : 16
* Unarmed (40%)
* Unnatural (80%)
* All other stats (30%)
##### His Phone
His phone is a pure obsidian box. It is not turned on, and it is the thing controlling the bus. On a successful unnatural roll, a player can use it to transport themselves and the bus to any dimension or place. On a successful (INT - 20%) roll, the players can go to the previous dimension or next dimension.  

##### Special Abilities
If prompted he will cast spells to defend himself. 
> **Shrimpify:**
> On a successful unnatural roll, he will turn the effected creature or item into a small dog sized shrimp. Note that this is extremely painful. This transformation only lasts for 1 minute. If an agent is threating him, he will turn them or their weapons into shrimp.


## Uh oh, where are we now?
After about 3-10 minutes (this can be decided by a dice roll or DM discretion) the bus will teleport to a new plane of existence / Location. This will continue until the players apprehend the bus driver or his phone. 

The bus will go to the following dimensions in order. Swiping up on the phone will bring you down one dimension, and up will bring you down one dimension. Swiping down while in the endless beach will being you to Spokane, and swiping up while in the shopping mall will also bring you back to Spokane. 

__On each location change__
* Roll Sanity ( 1d6 fail ... loose 1 on success)
### 1. The Endless Beach
Sunlight fills the bus windows. The bus doors open and briny sand sloshes into the bus. The sky is grey, yellow sand, and small briny pools of water stretch for as long as you can see. There's a semi-dense fog blocking the long distance view.  

> __Searching or looking outside__
> You can see tall (2 story) things walking around in the fog. Its hard to make out what they are. 

> __Getting left behind__
> In the event that a player is left behind in his location, one of the tall creatures will come over to interact with them. 

> __Tall Fella__
> A tall 2 story man made of twisted rebar and road signs. They speak all languages, but only talk in a customer service like voice. It always refers to you as "valued customer".
__Stats__
HP : 50
* Charisma (40%)
* Unnatural (80%)
* All other stats (30%)
____

1. First Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, and 30% of your toes.
2. Second Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, and a foot.
3. Third Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, a foot, and 10% of your grey matter.
4. If the player haggles to much or tries to kill him repeatedly, he will simply attack with a bite (75% 1d20), until they die.

### 2. Falling forever
Nothing, just inky blackness outside of the bus, every single player gets the impression of falling. 

> __Getting off the bus__
> If a player gets off the bus without a tether, they will gently float away. Them and the bus are "falling" at the same speed. Have the player roll either INT of SAN to force themselves to step outside. 

> __Getting left behind__
> If an agent is left behind, they will fall contentiously in inky blackness until they starve to death.


### 3. Shrimp Town



![[human-shrimp.png]]


*"I made it take ya to shrimp town"* - The bus driver says this out loud while giggling. 

An reddish glow fills the bus, and you look outside to see ... lots and lots of [[Human Sized Shrimp]]. There are small wooden huts around, as well as a campfire, where several human sized shrimp are roasting smaller, normal sized shrimp on a fire. 

A shrimp shaped crescent moon can be seen in the sky, with smaller orange lights were the stars are. 

> __Getting off the bus__
> The [[Human Sized Shrimp]] run away in terror. 

> __Getting left behind__
> Without teleportation, there is no way to get home from this dimension. But hey.... At least they have an endless supply of shrimp. 


### 4. Shopping mall
Quiet pleasant music plays, you can't identify what song it is, or what the words are. rows and rows of grocery isles full of toothpaste. stretch out in every direction. This is the cornerstone from [[6. Sketchway]] . 

The final stop on the bus, assuming the players do not stop the bus driver until then. The Bus driver will get off after this stop, and leave without saying anything. 


> __Getting off the bus__
> 1-2 [[Hungry Mannequin]]s will attack the player in 1-6 minutes if the players linger here. 

> __Getting left behind__
> If the bus driver somehow escapes the players and leaves them behind, the players will be trapped in the cornerstore from [[6. Sketchway]].
