## Hook
[[The Handler]] calls the players to a popular burger joint late at night. Normally he meets them in his retrofitted RV. As the agents arrive at the burger joint, they notice that the parking lot is empty except for a U-Haul, and another man in a dark tinted car, whom is playing on his phone.  

After sitting the players down, he admits the last couple of missions were a bit hectic, and he's hoping to give the players a safer, more simple mission for today. The handler will pay for whatever food the agents want.

### The original mission
The goal is to transport a box in a U-Haul from the parking lot to a self-storage container outside of city limits. Easy. The handler does not mention the contents of the box.

### An Interruption
The Handler then finishes his burger and takes the agents out to the parking lot to show them the U-Haul. Just as he steps out of the door a gunshot rings out. A small red dot appears on the handlers chest and he collapses. The handler screams in pain. The handler has been shot. 

## Current Objective
*"Get that son of a b!tch! and get me to a god damn hospital!"*

## The Man with the Gun

A man wearing a black hoodie and a ski mask is sitting in the drivers eat of a dodge charger. He is holding a russian SVD. If any of the players are in sight, he'll try to take a quick parting shot that them, and then attempt to drive away. He is the same person that the agents saw waiting in the parking lot.

__Stats:__

HP: 16
- Firearms : 60%  --- 1d12 (SVD)
- Drive    : 50% 
- Computer science : 80%
- All other stats 50%


> **Giving Chase:**
>    Giving chase is difficult, but the characters could pursue him in the U-Haul or a personal car. This will create a car chase. This means the driver of each car must contest their drive rolls. If the players fail three drive rolls in a row, the loose the assassin. If either driver critical fails, they crash their car. 


![[night-drive.png]]

> **Crashing:**
>   Each agent rolls intelligence to see if they remembered their seatbelt in the event of a crash. If succeed, take 1d8 damage, if fail, agents take 1d12. The assassin remembered his seatbelt, and will take only 1d8 damage if crashing.
> If the assassin survives the crash he will attempt to kill the agents pursuing with his gun.

>**Capturing:**
> If agents manage to capture the assassin, he will scream conspiring nonsense, about how the government is mind controlling birds, and how capitalism is controlled by a hidden wizard cult. He will not reveal how he tracked the handler down unless you interrogate it out of him.

> **Interrogating**
> On a successful charisma roll, he'll reveal that he had been tracking the handler and "his blonde deamon", through a hacked backdoor on the public traffic cameras. 

## The handler 
[[The Handler]] is bleeding out, very fast. He will die unless given immediate medical attention or taken to a hospital. Please note, if the handler dies, the game ends. 

> **How is he feeling?**
> The handler has been reduced to 8/16 hp. He is bleeding out at -1hp per turn. If someone passes a medical check on him, he will stabilize, and stop bleeding out. 

> **If stabilized:**
>     If you stabilized the handler he will attempt to make a call on his cell phone, he will then give to the GPS co-ordantes to the [[Handlers RV]], where he will meet a waiting [[Agent Cotton]], who will patch him up, if possible. 

## Meeting with agent cotton
If the agents get away they will meet a bleary eyed, very sleep deprived [[Agent Cotton]]. She has parked the handlers RV in the middle of the woods. She will escort the handler to the RV, where she will operate surgery on him. Agents are permitted inside the RV. 

## Completing the delivery
If the agents secure the handler, and capture / kill the would be assassin, they may desire to complete the delivery assigned to them. This part of the mission is entirely optional.

> **The self storage locker:**
> The keys to the U-Haul are in the front seat. There is also a key to a self storage locket outside of city limits.

> **The rattling box in the back:**
> The back of the truck contains a [[Angel of Desire]] trapped in a nailed shut wooden box. This box will rattle and shake every time the agents walk near it.
