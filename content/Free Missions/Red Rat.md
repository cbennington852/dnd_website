## Hook
[[The Handler]] has located the traitor that exposed his location in a previous mission ([[Red Alert]]). Considered armed an extremely dangerous.  Hunt him down and eliminate him.

Things given to players
* Note with GPS coordinates point to a house in the Ballard neighborhood Seattle.
* A photograph featuring a man wearing a vest, with multiple scars on arm, and a brick in a holster with the words "Agent Hemlock" written on the back. 
* [[Agent Cotton]] will reluctantly accompany the players. She is scared of agent hemlock. 

## Agent Hemlocks house
A small red brick house in Ballard Seattle, this is a low-middle class neighborhood, with cracked sidewalks and confusing streets. 

### The Front door
The front door opens up to a stairway that leads down and a ground floor. The stairway down is booby trapped. 
* Damage ... 1d12
* Alertness (-20%) to notice trap
* Demolitions to disarm or you can step over the trip wire. 

### Ground floor
There is a small living room with a TV, and beer spilled all over the couch. 
* Kitchen is stocked with beer and TV diners. 
* Small drab bedroom with little inside of it.
* Search -> A small active wireless camera located on top of the fridge. It is turned on.
### Basement
Multiple guitars are racked along the wall. 

There is a small desk, with two tickets receipts on top of it. 
- Eastbound train going to Spokane with the train name "Western Osprey" circled in red ink. 
- Westbound ferry ticket going to San Juan islands, with the boat name "Western Osprey" circled in red ink.

A successful search roll reveals a hidden box under the floor boards. It has
* Stone Knife
* Handwritten note in English. The handwriting is spidery, and its written in charcoal.
	* "If Human need escape, go to human vessel Western Osprey."

Both the train and ferry depart about a hour or two from the agents current location. 
- 30 min drive to get to either.
