Below is a series of free missions. You can play them in any order, but the recommended order is likely best. This follows a Redline group based on the pacific northwest. 

### Introduction and Background
Each player was working as a normal, government worker, until one day their supervisor asked them if they want to be a part of something bigger. Not a public role like a police officer, but a  secretive role for a mysterious organization. All of you agents accepted this offer. Each agent was given a cheap flip phone, and told to wait for a call.
