## Hook
[[The Handler]] calls the team to the [[Handlers RV]], and the handler instructs them that they need to escort [[Agent Cotton]] to the cornerstore to get her medication. He hands the players a brown leather wallet. [[Agent Cotton]] already knows where to go, and it wearing a hiking backpack.

## Objectives
Acquire Agent Cotton's medication.

### Inside the leather wallet
There are three white milky coins, each has a smile etched into each one.

> **Identifying the coins:**
> - Successful Unnatural Roll: They are a form of wizard currency.
> - Successful Forensics Roll: They are make of human bone. Roll sanity for 1d4.

# The Store

The store is in the middle of the sketchy side of downtown Spokane. There are several homeless milling around the building. The cornerstone is clearly shutdown, and surrounded by razor wire fences. It has several graffiti tags on the side of the building. The door to the building is not locked.

![[cornerstore.png]]

Entering the building, the players will be greeted with several racks of vitamins. Agent Cotton knows which way to go by a small map on her phone. Her phone is on 3% battery. 

The entrance that the players walked through on the real world connects to the larger "shopping dimension". This creates a "bigger on the inside effect". Upon realizing this any player must roll sanity, and take 1d4 san loss on success, and 1d10 sanity loss on failure. 

The corner store is enormous, spanning over 200 total miles of shelves and items. Items on shelves do not decay, and do not perish. 

Isles are given near nonsensical names such as "Vitamin D 56K". Isle names are also given nonsensical names such as "Loafs of Loaf AX7".

#### Items on the shelves

Items on the shelf span multiple different languages, however the pictures and advertisements on them seem to blur together. Expiration dates also given non-sense names such as "August -3 202A".

#### Things in the mall

If the players make too much noise, or walk for more than 2 hours, roll luck. If under 60%, two [[Hungry Mannequin]]s or [[Impersonator Mannequin]]s are nearby. If given the chance, they will attempt to attack the players and eat their personal information.

![[best_cornerstore.png]]

### Dave's shop
Outside of the circular store, the isles and racks simply end, opening up to long near infinite stretches of open warehouse. There are no mannequins here. Players can see Dave's shop in the distance from a long ways away. It appears to be a log cabin. There is a small drive through window in which Dave is standing.

#### The man himself
Wearing a wizard hat, cloak, and ski gloves. Dave is insane and erratic, his only goal is to make deals with the players. 

#### The exchange
Upon receiving the 3 silver dollars, Dave will abruptly vanish, and then return 5-10 seconds later. His beard will look longer, and he will be wearing different clothes. He will hand Agent Cotton an unmarked brown paper bag. Dave and Agent Cotton have met before, he does not try to make any deals with her. 

#### Bargaining
After buying the medicine, Dave will try to sell the players on exchanging their hair for various items. These can be any variation of nonsensical items, like for example "double phone, giraffe legs". 

> **Making a deal with Dave:**
> Dave will ask for percentages of the players "hair" in exchange for things. He says "I want hair", but he is not thinking of human hair. He wants the players lungs.
> | Item                | Cost                                                                          | Example                         |
> | ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
> | Minor magical item  | 20% of your "hair".<br>Permanent reduction of that players consituion by 2d6. | A phone with infinite bandwith. |
> | Medium magical item | 40% of you "hair".<br>Permanent reduction of the players constitution by 3d6. | A bulletproof shirt.            |
