
___Description___
* Short. 4 ft
* Grey skin
* Red eyes
* Rock hardhat. 

##### Sanity
Seeing one of these for the first time requires a sanity check 
Success -> 0 sanity lost
Failure -> 1d6 sanity lost

__Behavior___
These creatures do not talk, they communicate telepathically, and have no reason to speak to humans. Hostile. Will attack. 

These are the "civilian" versions of the shog's, and they are often tasked with maintaining the under dark infrastructure. 

___Stats___
5hp
* Swing Tool / Hammer (60%) 1d6
* Teleport  (2 uses) (Needs a 5 minute recharge. )
