These are human-like statues, their body is frozen in place, however they can still do a sort of "gliding action."

These "creatures" simply want to be as close to a human as possible. They will "glide" toward the closest person that they can see. The further away, the faster they will glide.

#### High Speeds
These statues will accelerate toward the nearest person, they accelerate at roughly the speed of a car, with an upper speed capacity of 900 MPH. This acceleration is uniform. 

###### Ramming Damages

| Accelration                   | Damage      |
| ----------------------------- | ----------- |
| < 15 sec acceleration         | 0 damage    |
| 15 -30 sec accelration        | 1d4 damage  |
| 30 sec - 1min of acceleration | 1d6 damage  |
| 1 - 2 mins acceleration       | 1d8 damage  |
| 2-3 mins acceleration         | 1d10 damage |
| 3+ mins acceleration          | 1d20 damage |
