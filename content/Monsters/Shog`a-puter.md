___Description___
* Avg. 5 ft
* Grey skin
* Red eyes
* Fused with a rdesk / computer made of rocks. They are immobile. 
* They have small t-rex like arms that can type on the computer in front of them.

##### Sanity
Seeing one of these for the first time requires a sanity check 
Success -> 0 sanity lost
Failure -> 1d6 sanity lost

__Behavior___
These creatures talk, and are essentially computers. If able, they will summon security. They can summon trains(if in a train station), and otherwise manage the communications and administrative tasks for the Shogs.

__The computer__
There are two parts of the keyboard. It is in another language. 
* Unnatural ... You can read part of the computer.
