## Turns
Agents and Monsters both take turns during combat. Unless otherwise stated a agent or monster has one action per turn. A turn is about 8 seconds. 

## Initiative 
The person who rolls first is determined by a combination of alertness and dexterity. 

### Being ambushed
If Agents is being ambushed or attacked, they must roll alertness, if they get a chance to use an action before they are attacked. 

### Dexterity
To determine the rolling order, agents will roll against their dexterity. The order is determined by how much you pass your dexterity roll by. Creatures or agent who fail dexterity skip their first turn, and take their actions at the start of the next turn. 

## Guns

### Aimed Shots

If agents are using a gun, they can sacrifice an action to take the "aim" action. Taking the aim action skips their turn, but means their next firearms roll is at a +25% advantage. 

### Specific Shots

Agents can take a -40% penalty to their firearms skill to aim for a small specific part of the thing they are aiming at. This doubles the damage of their shot.


## Cars

### Car Crash

Car crashes happen in tiers. Each tier depends on how fast you are traveling, and what you are hitting. DM's can decide what tier a car crash is. Generally speaking, if a player critical fails a drive roll, that is usually a medium car crash.

| Type             | Speed Range    | Scenarios                                                                  | Vehicle Status                        | Saving Roll  | Damage                                |
| ---------------- | -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Minor Accident   | below 15 mph   | Bumping another car. Parking lot accident. Hitting a deer.                 | Car operational.                      | Constitution | 0 Damage on a pass.<br>1d4 on a fail. |
| Average Accident | 15 mpg - 40mph | Crashing the car into a building. T-boning at an intersection going 30mph. | Car requires engineering roll to fix. | Constitution | 1d4 on a pass.<br>1d8 on a fail.      |
| Major Accident   | + 40mph        | Head on collision. Highway speed accident.                                 | Car destroyed                         | Constitution | 1d8 on a pass.<br>1d12 on a fail.     |

### Ramming things with cars

Ramming things with a car is essentially a controlled car crash. It is highly dependent on how heavy the object is that you are hitting. When you ram something with a car, it deals double car crash damage to the thing being rammed, and standard car crash damage to the driver. 

> **Example:**
> Agent Pine attempts to run over the deamon in the parking lot. Agent Pine is going about 30mph. Agent Pine passes his drive roll. Agent Pine fails his constitution roll, taking 1d8 damage. The deamon fails it's constitution roll taking 1d8 damage.

*Note: If two cars try to ram each other at the same time, both drivers take double damage.*


## Going down
If an agents HP is reduced to zero, they enter a downed state. They cannot move, and are bleeding out. The agent requires medical attention soon. The difficulty required to revive them will increase over time until they die. A successful first aid roll will revive them. 

| Turns without medical Aid | Offset                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| 1                         | +10% (Slightly Easier)   |
| 2                         | 0% (Normal)              |
| 3                         | -10% (Slightly Harder)   |
| 4                         | -30% (Difficult)         |
| 5                         | -50% (Very Hard)         |
| 6                         | -70% (Nearly Impossible) |
| 7                         | Agent dies               |


## Critical Hits
Critical hits result in an attack doing double damage.
