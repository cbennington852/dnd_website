
## Welcome to Redline



## Who are your Agents?

Agents are players. These are people who have been brought into Redline, usually at the recommendation of a friendly supervisor. Your Agents agreed to be a part of the Redline organization, and are now on the hook 24/7 for protecting the world against unnatural threats. Agents are not paid per say, but if they make it to retirement, the agents will be rewarded with a large lump sum of money, tucked away in an investment account for them.  

Overtime this exposure to the unnatural world will eat away at agent's [[Sanity]], and sometimes even augment their bodes. Agents will loose or gain (mostly loose) sanity by their actions in game. This sanity loss will overtime manifest as [[Mental Disorders]], altering character behavior and abilities.

## The handler and Missions

The handler (The DM), is a physical character who delegates missions to the players. This person can be whoever you want them to be. This handler is backed by the government, and has access to all police records, government security cameras, and other discrete methods of gathering information. They can bail your Agents out of trouble, call them, and provide forged documents. 

There is however, one stipulation. If the handler is killed or otherwise incapacitated, the campaign ends. No more missions. All Agents return to being normal citizens or are killed. Due to this reason, handlers usually live in seclusion, and leave the [[Combat]] and the risky [[Rolling Dice|dice rolls]] to the agents.
