

  

Drastic actions are a special kind of home scene where an agent does something desperate or out of the ordinary to ensure an event occurs. Performing a drastic measure consumes a home scene. 


| Name                     | Description                                                            | Effects                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Heavy Anti-Phycotics     | An agent acquires heavy antipsychotic medication from a psychiatrist.  | The Agent Restores (1d20+4) of sanity. The Agent also reduces their Intelligence, Dexterity, and Strength by 1d10 permanently.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Credit Card Debt         | An Agent goes into substantial high interest debt to cover an expense. | During a home scene, decease the difficulty of an active purchasing roll by 20. This can be done after seeing the result of your initial finance roll. The Agent takes 1d10 sanity damage, and reduces their finances permanently by 20. If their finances go into the negative, they take 1d20 of sanity damage. |
| Relocation to psych ward | An agent is admitted to a pysch ward to treat a mental illness.        | An agent takes  reduces their finances and charisma by 1d20. They cure one mental illness. They can leave the psych ward to execute missions. If their finances go into the negative, they take 1d20 of sanity damage. This consumes a single home scene.                                                                                            |
