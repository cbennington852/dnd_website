
| Stat Name    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Strength     | A creatures brute strength. This is general measure of how much raw pounds per square inch a creature can output.<br><br>*Example: "I would like to smash that door down."*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Constitution | A general measure of how tough or durable a creature is. Note that player health is determined by how high their Constitution is.<br><br>*Example: "Your mouth tastes like copper. Roll Constitution. "*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Dexterity    | A general measure of how fast or responsive a creature is when compared to other creatures.<br><br>*Example: "I would like to shoot that terrorist before he shoots me."*                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Intelligence | A general measure of how prepared a character is. This is also a measure of how well a creature can remember things.<br><br>1. **Remembering a Cheap Item:** Players can roll Intelligence to "remember" to bring a cheap item(under 50$). <br><br>*Example: "Roll Intelligence to see if you have a flashlight."*<br><br>2. **Asking the DM a question:** Players can also use Intelligence to ask the DM a basic or relatively common question.<br><br>*Example: "Would I get hurt jumping out of this moving car?"*<br> |
| Power        | Generally speaking, this denotes the amount of determination a character has. Note that player starting sanity and breaking point is determined by how high their Power is.<br><br>*Example: "You can hear the voice of Shog-meail in your ear. Roll to resist the urge to lick the ancient totem."*                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Charisma     | A general measure of how good a creature is at interacting with humans, and socializing. This can be used in several ways, but below are the most common.<br><br>1. **Convincing others:** Players can use this to convince other humans to do things for them.<br><br>*Example: "I would like to convince the bystander to leave."*<br><br>2.  **Determining Emotions:** Players can use charisma to gain insight into how another human might be feeling.  <br><br>*Example: "Is she lying?"*                            |
