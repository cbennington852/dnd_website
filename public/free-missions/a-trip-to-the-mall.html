May 31, 20264 min read

Hook

The Handler calls the team to the Handlers RV, and the handler gives them a “Shopping list”, as well as an airtight metal container(25lb). The players arrive around 6pm.

  • Players get 3 silver dollars.
  • Players get a compass labeled (Dave)
  • Agent Cotton gets 1 silver dollar.
  • Agent Cotton gets a compass labelled (Barbershop)
  • Agent Cotton has a hikers backpack, filled with food and drink, as well as a compass.

Shopping List

  • Go to the mall (GPS coordinates attached, that lead to an abandoned mall on the outskirts of spokane valley … 47.6733948484444, -117.20771045207604)
    • Locate Dave
    • Convert the contents into .MP4 format. Do not open yourselves.
    • Take agent cotton to the barbershop
    • Get agent cotton an haircut

Inside the box Inside the box is the severed head of the wizard killed on the previous mission (Doomscrolling). It is on ice. His eyes are gouged out.

  • Looking inside … roll Sanity (1d4)

The Mall

The mall is an old abandoned mall on the outskirts of Spokane valley. It is surrounded by a chain link fence, and you can see graffiti on the outside of the mall building. There is a missing person poster stapled to the street post outside of the mall.

Entering the mall This must be done at night, otherwise the gate glyph will not work. Players need to cut through the heavy chain link fence first.

Inside

Quiet pleasant music plays, you can’t identify what song it is, or what the words are. rows and rows of clothing (all types) stretch out in every direction.

There are mannequins modeling on clothes, the clothes have price tags on them, but the prices aren’t in English.

For every 1 mile walked, subtract 5 years from the style of the clothes, this starts at 2025, and ends at 1960. Every 8 hrs, the lights will either turn on or off.

The Barbershop

Located about 8 miles (1985 styled clothes) out, in the southeast direction. There is a small clearing, with a strange metallic machine sitting in a semi-circle.

The Machine Looks like a spider, with scissors in place of it’s mandibles. There is a single barber chair in front of it, and a coin slot on the chair. Still. Unnmoving. This is made of metal

Agent Cotton haircut Agent cotton with happily walk up to the chair, take off her backpack.

  • Note: If any of the players have a cursed item, she will try to convince them to let her hold it, so she can use it in exchange as currency.

After inserting the coin, the machine will whir to life, with a loud screeching sound. It will then start cutting agent cottons hair (Agent cotton will fall unconscious).

  • Agent Cotton restores 2d20 of sanity.
  • This may trigger the Hungry Mannequins to attack. Depends on how players doing.

Dave’s Shop

Located 13.5 miles out, in the void

Following into the empty warehouse space, after a short while, you see a clearly homemade wooden house. The outside has a sign that says “Dave’s shop”.

The shopkeeper(Dave) meets you at a small drive though window on the side of the poorly constructed house.

Dave

Wearing a wizard hat, cloak, and ski gloves. Dave is insane and erratic, constantly trying to sell you products that don’t exist.

The exchange Upon receiving the 3 silver dollars, Dave will abruptly vanish, and then return 5-10 seconds later. His beard will look longer, and he will be wearing different clothes. He will hand the players a standard thumb drive with 1tb of space.

Stats 16hp Dave can attack twice

  • Magic Missile (80%) 1d10
  • Teleport (90%)
  • Time Travel (70%)
  • Unarmed (50%)

Mannequin Interactions

This can happen eventually, to both groups, or one group, if players decide to stick together.

Note: If a mannequin escapes, this will cause a catastrophe. (From them eating people and replicating. )
