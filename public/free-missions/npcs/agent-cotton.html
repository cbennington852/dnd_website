Agent Cotton

Agent Cotton

Aug 14, 20268 min read

A young blonde woman. She wears an FBI vest, and and an automatic UZI in her right gun holster. She sounds a little bit insane. Also a tiny bit forgetful. She is a Redline friendly who occasionally accompanies players on missions.

Special Skills and Abilities

Curse of the grimwalker: Agent Cotton was cursed a long time ago on a previous mission. If Agent Cotton perishes, she will come back to life the next day with little to no memory how how she died. Each time she dies, her sanity goes down by 1d20.
