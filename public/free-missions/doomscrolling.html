My Secret Note

May 31, 20266 min read

Hook

A suspected Wizard was flagged to have boarded a Metro Bus(east bound Division 25 stop 3 @ 1:23 am). Bring him in alive.

The Bus

The players board the bus around 1:23am. It is cold and gloomy, the only light around them is from streetlights. The players board the bus somewhere in downtown Spokane.

People on the bus

As the players get on the bus you can see there are two total people on the bus… Well, three if you count the bus driver.

The Grumpy Elderly Man

A grumpy elderly man, will snarl at anyone nearby. He is nearly black out drunk, and reeks of alcohol. He is spread out, taking up two bus seats in the disabled section. You can see two bottles of beer under his left arm. Description Elderly white male, wearing a brown jacket. Stats HP : 7

  • Revolver (60% firearms) 1d8
  • Unarmed (40%)
  • All other stats (30%)

The bird lover

A young male. His is watching hentai on his phone, with the phone speaker on. He is also wearing a red angry birds hat. Description Young white male. Grey hoodie wearing a red angry birds hat. Stats HP : 0

  • Unarmed (40%)
  • All other stats (30%)

The Bus Driver

The bus driver is not paying attention, and is looking down and is scrolling on his phone. He does not acknowledge the players as they get on the bus.

Description Middle aged white male. Balding. Wearing a bus drivers uniform. Stats HP : 13

  • Unarmed (40%)
  • Unnatural (80%)
  • All other stats (30%)
His Phone

His phone is a pure obsidian box. It is not turned on, and it is the thing controlling the bus. On a successful unnatural roll, a player can use it to transport themselves and the bus to any dimension or place. On a successful (INT - 20%) roll, the players can go to the previous dimension or next dimension.

Spells

If prompted he will cast spells to defend himself. He has access to

  • Mind Stop III
  • Mind Control II from Scholar of mushroom

Uh oh, where are we now?

After about 3-10 minutes (this can be decided by a dice roll or DM discretion) the train will teleport to a new plane of existence / Location. This will continue until the players apprehend the bus driver or his phone.

The bus will go to the following dimensions in order. Swiping up on the phone will bring you down one dimension, and up will bring you down one dimension. Swiping down while in the endless beach will being you to Spokane, and swiping up while in the shopping mall will also bring you back to Spokane.

On each location change

  • Roll Sanity ( 1d6 fail … loose 1 on success)

1. The Endless Beach

Sunlight fills the bus windows. The bus doors open and briny sand sloshes into the bus. The sky is grey, yellow sand, and small briny pools of water stretch for as long as you can see. There’s a semi-dense fog blocking the long distance view.

Searching or looking outside You can see tall (2 story) things walking around in the fog. Its hard to make out what they are.

Getting left behind In the event that a player is left behind in his location, one of the tall creatures will come over to interact with them.

Tall Fella A tall 2 story man made of twisted rebar and road signs. They speak all languages, but only talk in a customer service like voice. It always refers to you as “valued customer”. Stats HP : 50

  • Charisma (40%)
  • Unnatural (80%)
  • All other stats (30%)
  1. First Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, and 30% of your toes.
  2. Second Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, and a foot.
  3. Third Offer : Send you back to where you came, in exchange for all of your hair, a foot, and 10% of your grey matter.
  4. If the player haggles to much or tries to kill him repeatedly, he will simply attack with a bite (75% 1d20), until they die.

2. The Endless Void

Nothing, just inky blackness outside of the bus, every single player gets the impression of falling.

Getting off the bus If a player gets off the bus without a tether, they will gently float away. Them and the bus are “falling” at the same speed. Have the player roll either INT of SAN to force themselves to step outside.

Getting left behind If an agent is left behind, they will fall contentiously in inky blackness until they starve to death.

3. The Underground

The players step out into the The Underdark. Specifically a mushroom field for the tall men.

Getting off the bus The Shog’s will attack the players on sight, causing a combat scene.

Getting left behind If an agent is left behind, they will likely be overwhelmed by the shog defense forces. If not they could potentially navigate to the surface through one of the The Underdark tunnels to the surface.

4. Shrimp Town

“I made it take ya to shrimp town” - The bus driver says this out loud while giggling.

An reddish glow fills the bus, and you look outside to see … lots and lots of Human Sized Shrimp. There are small wooden huts around, as well as a campfire, where several human sized shrimp are roasting smaller, normal sized shrimp on a fire.

A shrimp shaped crescent moon can be seen in the sky, with smaller orange lights were the stars are.

Getting off the bus The Human Sized Shrimp run away in terror.

Getting left behind Without teleportation, there is no way to get home from this dimension. But hey… At least they have an endless supply of shrimp.

5. Shopping mall

Quiet pleasant music plays, you can’t identify what song it is, or what the words are. rows and rows of clothing (all types) stretch out in every direction. There are mannequins modeling on clothes, the clothes have price tags on them, but the prices aren’t in English.

The final stop on the bus, assuming the players do not stop the bus driver until then. The Bus driver will get off after this stop, and leave without saying anything.

Getting off the bus 1-2 Hungry Mannequins will attack the player in 1-6 minutes if the players linger here.

Getting left behind The bus will be about an mile from the exit to New York. This mall is endless, but there are entrance doors that take you to various places in the world. A successful INT or SEARCH roll can reveal an exit sign, which the players could follow.
