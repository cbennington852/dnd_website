Buying things.

Buying things requires good finances. Upon buying things, Agents are required to make a finance roll.

Purchasing items is done though a finance roll. If players desire to increase their finance, they can purchase an investment, or they can level up their finance skills.

Critical Success on finance: Player get the object they were looking for easily, and at a lower price than expected. The player also acquires a free “short term investment” item.

Success on finance: Player purchases item.

Failure on finance: Player is unable to purchase the item.

Critical Failure on finance: Player makes a major financial or logical burden. They do not get the item. What happens here is up to the DM’s discretion.
